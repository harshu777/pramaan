About Certificate Validation

Our certificate validation system uses blockchain technology and IPFS to ensure the authenticity and immutability of issued certificates. Each certificate is:

  • Secured with a unique cryptographic hash
  • Stored permanently on IPFS (InterPlanetary File System)
  • Anchored on the blockchain for tamper-proof verification
  • Verifiable in real-time through multiple methods

For support or questions, please contact the certificate issuing authority.
